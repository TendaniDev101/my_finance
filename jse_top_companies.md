# Top 100 Famous Brands and Companies Listed on the JSE

A curated overview of the most prominent, widely recognized companies and consumer brands listed on the Johannesburg Stock Exchange (JSE).

---

## 🏦 Banking & Financial Services
* **Standard Bank Group** (SBK) - Africa's largest lender by assets.
* **FirstRand** (FSR) - Financial group behind First National Bank (FNB).
* **Capitec Bank Holdings** (CPI) - Retail banking disruptor.
* **Absa Group** (ABG) - Major pan-African banking franchise.
* **Nedbank Group** (NED) - Big-four commercial and retail green bank.
* **Discovery Limited** (DSY) - Global health insurance innovator (Vitality).
* **Sanlam** (SLM) - Africa's largest insurance and wealth management group.
* **Old Mutual** (OMU) - Long-standing financial services and insurance giant.
* **OUTsurance Group** (OUT) - Popular direct short-term and personal insurer.
* **Investec Limited** (INL) - International specialist banking and wealth manager.
* **Remgro** (REM) - Diversified investment holding company.
* **Ninety One** (NY1) - Global asset manager demerged from Investec.
* **Coronation Fund Managers** (CML) - Independent asset manager.
* **Momentum Metropolitan Holdings** (MTM) - Large insurance and investment group.
* **PSG Financial Services** (KST) - Wealth management and financial planning network.

## 🛒 Retail, Consumer Goods & Food
* **Shoprite Holdings** (SHP) - Africa's largest grocery retailer (Checkers, Usave).
* **Woolworths Holdings** (WHL) - Upscale food, fashion, and beauty department store network.
* **Pick n Pay Stores** (PIK) - Core staple retail supermarket and clothing chain.
* **Boxer Retail** (BOX) - Rapidly expanding discount supermarket brand.
* **Mr Price Group** (MRP) - Value fashion, home, and sport retailer.
* **The Foschini Group / TFG** (TFG) - Lifestyle and clothing retail conglomerate.
* **Truworths International** (TRU) - Premium fashion and apparel retail group.
* **Pepkor Holdings** (PPH) - Operates high-volume discount chains (PEP, Ackermans).
* **Clicks Group** (CLS) - Leading pharmacy, health, and beauty retail chain.
* **Dis-Chem Pharmacies** (DCP) - Major national dispensary and wellness warehouse chain.
* **Tiger Brands** (TBS) - Fast-moving consumer goods behemoth (Jungle Oats, All Gold).
* **Famous Brands** (FBR) - Restaurant franchisor (Steers, Wimpy, Debonairs Pizza).
* **Motus Holdings** (MTS) - Automotive retail, rental, and import network.
* **Super Group** (SPG) - Supply chain, logistics, and fleet management operation.
* **Bid Corporation / Bidcorp** (BID) - International foodservice distribution provider.

## 🌐 Technology, Media & Telecoms
* **Naspers** (NPN) - Tech heavyweight linked to global consumer internet plays.
* **Prosus** (PRX) - Global consumer internet spin-off holding a major stake in Tencent.
* **MTN Group** (MTN) - Pan-African mobile network telecommunications giant.
* **Vodacom Group** (VOD) - Leading mobile network operator majority-owned by Vodafone.
* **Telkom SA** (TKG) - Integrated state-backed wireline and wireless telecom utility.
* **Blue Label Telecoms** (BLU) - Virtual distributor of electronic tokens and majority owner of Cell C.
* **Datatec** (DTC) - International networking and information technology service group.
* **Altron** (AEL) - Technology, electronics, and digital transformation service provider.

## ⛏️ Resources, Mining & Basic Materials
* **BHP Group** (BHG) - The world's largest diversified resources company.
* **Anglo American plc** (AGL) - Multinational mining group spanning platinum, diamonds, and base metals.
* **Glencore plc** (GLN) - Global commodity trading and natural resource producer.
* **AngloGold Ashanti** (ANG) - International gold mining enterprise.
* **Gold Fields** (GFI) - Globally diversified gold producer.
* **Harmony Gold Mining Co** (HAR) - Operator of deep-level gold mines across SA.
* **Impala Platinum / Implats** (IMP) - Global producer of platinum group metals.
* **Sibanye-Stillwater** (SSW) - Transnational precious metals mining group.
* **Anglo American Platinum / Amplats** (AMS) - World's largest primary producer of platinum.
* **Northam Platinum** (NPH) - Fully integrated independent PGM producer.
* **Exxaro Resources** (EXX) - Large diversified black-empowered resource entity.
* **African Rainbow Minerals** (ARM) - Diversified mining group founded by Patrice Motsepe.
* **Kumba Iron Ore** (KIO) - High-grade iron ore exporter owned by Anglo American.
* **Sappi** (SAP) - Global pulp and paper manufacturing multinational.
* **Mondi plc** (MNP) - International packaging and paper giant.

## 🏭 Industry, Energy & Infrastructure
* **Sasol Limited** (SOL) - Integrated energy, fuel, and chemical giant.
* **Bidvest Group** (BVT) - Diversified services, commercial trading, and logistics conglomerate.
* **Barloworld** (BAW) - Industrial distributor of heavy Caterpillar machinery.
* **Aspen Pharmacare Holdings** (APN) - Multinational specialty and branded pharmaceutical developer.
* **Adcock Ingram** (AIP) - Local pharmaceutical manufacture company.
* **Netcare Limited** (NTC) - Private hospital network operator.
* **Life Healthcare Group** (LHC) - Healthcare infrastructure and private facility group.
* **Mediclinic International** - Global private hospital group operating across SA and Europe.
* **PPC Limited** (PPC) - Supplier of cement and construction materials in Southern Africa.
* **Omnia Holdings** (OMN) - Diversified chemical group serving agriculture and mining.
* **AECI Limited** (AFE) - Explosives, chemicals, and industrial materials business.
* **Reunert** (RLO) - Electrical engineering, defense, and IT equipment manufacturer.
* **Raubex Group** (RBX) - Infrastructure development and civil engineering group.

## 💎 Luxury Goods & Cross-Listings
* **Compagnie Financière Richemont** (CFR) - Switzerland-based global luxury goods titan (Cartier).
* **Anheuser-Busch InBev / AB InBev** (ANH) - The largest consumer brewing conglomerate in the world.
* **British American Tobacco** (BTI) - Global cigarette and tobacco manufacturer.
* **Reinet Investments** (RNI) - Luxembourg-based investment vehicle.

## 🏢 Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs)
* **Growthpoint Properties** (GRT) - Largest primary commercial REIT listed on the JSE.
* **Redefine Properties** (RDF) - Diversified property portfolio owner (office, retail, industrial).
* **NEPI Rockcastle** (NRP) - Commercial property investor focusing heavily on Central/Eastern Europe.
* **Hyprop Investments** (HYP) - Specialist shopping center REIT (Canal Walk, Somerset Mall).
* **Attacq Limited** (ATT) - Capital-growth property fund behind Waterfall City.
* **Vukile Property Fund** (VKE) - Retail-focused property management fund.
* **Resilient REIT** (RES) - Regional retail mall owner and developer.
* **Fairvest Limited** (FTA) - Property fund focusing on retail centers in non-metropolitan areas.
* **Fortress Real Estate Investments** (FFB) - Logistics, industrial, and retail real estate fund.
* **SA Corporate Real Estate** (SAC) - Industrial, retail, and residential asset fund.

## 🎭 Travel, Leisure & Entertainment
* **Tsogo Sun** (TSH) - Hotel, casino, and hospitality entertainment management group.
* **Southern Sun** (SSU) - Hospitality brand and hotel network across Africa.
* **Sun International** (SUI) - Premium resort and casino operator (Sun City).
* **City Lodge Hotels** (CLH) - Select-service business and leisure hotel group.
* **MultiChoice Group** (MCG) - Video entertainment operator (DStv, Showmax).
